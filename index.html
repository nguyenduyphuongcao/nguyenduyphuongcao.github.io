Pricing Without Scale

Independent research, 2026

Why do independent hotels hold their rates flat while chains move theirs daily?

I built a conceptual model combining Littlewood's two-class rule with dual entitlement and price fairness, estimating an illustrative 11.7% foregone yield under static pricing, then looked at why the gap survives.

The answer was not technology. A chain that raises its rates looks like it is following policy. An independent doing the same looks like it is taking advantage. The paper closes with a scale-light revenue management architecture, built on rate fences and protection-level rules, for operators too small to run a full system.
